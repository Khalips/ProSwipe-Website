THE PROBLEM

Finding the right opportunity shouldn't be this difficult.

Students often have to search through university websites, prospectuses, bursary platforms, and application portals just to determine which opportunities they qualify for.

01

Too Many Platforms

University programmes, bursaries, requirements, and deadlines are often scattered across multiple websites and resources.

02

Confusing Requirements

APS scores, subject requirements, minimum marks, NBT requirements, and other eligibility criteria can be difficult to understand and compare.

03

Missed Opportunities

Students may overlook suitable programmes or bursaries simply because they never discover them or do not realise that they qualify.

04

Too Much Searching

Finding and comparing relevant opportunities can take hours of searching through prospectuses, websites, and application pages.

THE SOLUTION

One platform. Opportunities matched to you.

ProSwipe brings student opportunities together in one simple platform. By using relevant information from a student's profile, ProSwipe aims to identify university programmes, bursaries, and opportunities that best match their academic standing, interests, and needs.

01

Personalised Matching

Opportunities are filtered using relevant information such as academic results, subjects, APS, NBT results, interests, and other eligibility criteria.

02

Clear Eligibility

Students can quickly understand why an opportunity may suit them instead of manually comparing their profile against every individual requirement.

03

Simple Discovery

Relevant opportunities are presented in an easy-to-browse format, allowing students to explore, save, and access the information they need more efficiently.

HOW IT WORKS

From your profile to the right opportunity.

ProSwipe simplifies the process of discovering suitable opportunities by guiding students through four simple steps.

01

Build Your Profile

Add relevant information such as your subjects, academic results, APS score, NBT results, interests, and other details that may affect your eligibility.

02

Get Matched

ProSwipe compares your profile with available opportunities and identifies programmes, bursaries, and services that may be suitable for you.

03

Swipe & Explore

Browse personalised suggestions through a simple swipe-based interface and quickly decide which opportunities interest you.

04

Save & Apply

Save opportunities that interest you and access the relevant official information when you are ready to continue with an application.

THE BIGGER PICTURE

Starting with students. Built for more.

ProSwipe begins by helping students discover university programmes, bursaries, and relevant opportunities. As the platform grows, the same personalised matching approach can expand into other areas of service discovery.

ABOUT PROSWIPE

Built around a simple idea: finding the right opportunity should be easier.

ProSwipe is a privately founded technology company created to simplify the way people discover opportunities and services that are relevant to them.

The company begins with a student-focused platform that helps learners and university students discover suitable programmes, bursaries, and other opportunities. As ProSwipe develops, the same personalised matching approach is intended to expand into broader areas of service discovery.

01

Privately Founded

ProSwipe was established by a four-member founding team responsible for the strategic, financial, operational, and technological direction of the company.

02

Student-First

Our initial focus is making university programmes, bursaries, and student opportunities easier to discover and understand.

03

Built to Expand

ProSwipe is designed around a broader vision of personalised service discovery that can extend beyond the student market over time.

OUR VISION

A future where finding the right service feels effortless.

ProSwipe's vision is to become a leading multidisciplinary service-discovery platform that connects users with services best suited to their individual profiles, preferences, and needs.

While our journey begins with students, the long-term goal is to apply the same personalised matching approach across a broader range of sectors, creating one convenient and accessible platform for discovering relevant services.
